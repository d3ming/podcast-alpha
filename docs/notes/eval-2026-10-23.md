# Eval: pipeline run and the Oct 21-23 daily reports (2026-10-23)

Scope: one live `daily --no-transcribe` run (48-hour window, output to a scratch dir), and the three Notion daily reports
(2026-10-21, -22, -23, 9 picks). Reports were checked against YouTube captions fetched from a residential Mac with
`youtube-transcript-api` (6 of the 9 picks have a YouTube video; the Jassy video was found by `yt-dlp` search).
Not checked: the Pioneers of AI (Friar, Oct 22) pick, speaker attribution (captions carry no speaker labels), whether the
picks were the best 3 of the day.

## Pipeline run (confirmed)

- 48 episodes in 75 s, 0 source errors, 6 with a transcript, 42 needing whisper. Triage picked 16 for whisper (top 8 plus core people).
- All three Oct 23 picks were in the candidate set: triage rank 1 (Jassy), 6 (a16z Frontier), 13 (Chamath).
- The Oct 22 All-In pick (Krishna and Arora) ranked 23 and would not have been whispered: the title names two CEOs but has no
  title keyword and neither is in `people.yaml`. Triage can drop an episode the agent later picks; TMTB recall is the only net.
- Rerunning `daily` with the same `--run-name` took `manifest.json` from 48 episodes to 0 (all deduped). Fixed: `fetch` now merges into an existing manifest, and `manifest.update` serialises all writers.

## Report quality (confirmed against captions)

Roughly 40 cited claims across 8 of 9 picks: about 32 land within a minute of the cited time with matching content, 4 are
wrong, 4 carry a systematic offset.

- Good: numbers and framing mostly hold (Jassy 76 days, $25B run rate, $0.03 vs $10 per fix, 27 s / 29 min breakouts, 30 states, 2032 IP).
  Interests-to-note and hedged attribution ("who the hosts say has nearly 6 GW") are well done.
- Wrong number: "about 15 GW of chips waiting for liquid-cooled data centers" (Blundin, 0:45:51). Captions say 50 gigawatts, and
  say Elon said it.
- Conflated cite: one 0:42:21 bullet merges the "6 GW" (said at 0:33:50 and 0:39:36) and "100 GW by 2030" (0:49:05).
- Misplaced timestamps (Pichai, Oct 21): "overshoot" cited at 0:25:50, said at 0:29:03; the "three-year planning horizon" is cited
  at 0:22:47 in the signal and 0:26:46 in the section; it is at 0:22:50.
- Offset (Friar, Oct 21): cites run 1.0-1.6 minutes later than the linked YouTube video. The timestamps come from the podcast
  audio, the link goes to the video. Nothing tells the reader.
- Unit passed through: "US 1.4 TWh short by 2050 (about six Californias)" repeats the speaker's units without a flag; they do not agree.
- Links: the Oct 23 Jassy pick links to Apple although the a16z YouTube video exists (`-YMMRQ1qscI`, 3639 s). The links step
  has no search path that finds it (DuckDuckGo is rate-limited, the show has no `youtube_channel_id`).
- Selection: Oct 23 has two picks from The a16z Show and Ben Horowitz is in both (host of one, guest of the other).
- Format drift: Oct 21 and 22 pages have no `Podcast Intel:` title line and no timestamp deep links; Oct 23 has both.

## What this implies

- A range or verbatim check cannot see the real failures; a number-near-timestamp check can (`check` and `report` now print these as warnings).
- Timestamps can only be trusted against the transcript the agent read. For picks with a YouTube link, re-anchoring against
  captions (works from the Mac, the box is likely blocked) would remove the offset class.

## Weekly top-15 dogfood (confirmed unless marked)

Ran `fetch --days 7` over a 7-day window with the daily lists: 94 episodes, 42 with a transcript. 52 had none because whisper
is not installed on this Mac. Four parallel readers scored the 42 and extracted claims; the coordinator merged them,
dropped one duplicate (Thiel on two feeds) and ranked 41 by score, tier, recency (`prompts/rank.md`).

- Spread: 4 episodes at 8, median 5, 18 of 41 at 4 or below. Within the rubric.
- Grounding: `publish` now runs the daily verifier on `ranked.json`. First pass flagged one error and three warnings:
  one false positive in the checker (a16z read as the number 16), one near-verbatim claim, two claims at the wrong timestamp.
  Claims were fixed and the checker regex was corrected; the final pass has 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- Spot check of four top claims against the transcripts (Jassy $25B run rate and sold-out capacity, Pichai 25T tokens in
  72 hours, FCF-negative framing): all match. Not checked: the remaining claims beyond the machine checks, and scores.
- Friction found and fixed: the digest listed all 41 episodes with claims (53 KB); `publish --top 15` now gives 15 (22 KB).
  Empty episode URLs rendered as `[title]()`; they are now plain titles.
- Open at the time, since addressed: show homepages as episode URLs (the digest now prints a `Show:` line from `show_links.yaml` and a
  `Transcript:` link; `page_url` also tries the feed guid), the same person twice (Pichai at #3 and #9, two interviews, left as is).
- Still open: 52 episodes unranked for lack of a transcript, so the ranking is biased toward shows with feed transcripts or YouTube
  captions. Re-running triage on the Oct 21-23 daily run with the new two-guest rule moves Krishna and Arora from rank 23 to 22
  of 48, still outside the 8 that get whispered; Dwarkesh (rank 10) and the CrowdStrike pick (rank 9) were also outside. On the Mac
  `--youtube-mirror` covers these; on the box the whisper budget is the limit.
- Speed: a live `daily --no-transcribe` took 75 s sequentially and 43 s with feeds fetched 8 at a time. The rest is feedparser
  parsing large feeds on one core.
